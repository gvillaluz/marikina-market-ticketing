# Scanning, calculation and verification — criterion 4, offense position and calculated fine (closed 2026-11-10)

Closed by the user on 2026-11-10 after the automated checks, the deliberate breaks and the live check on the phone passed, and pull request #12 was merged (merge commit `908eb5e`). Full results: `evidence/fine-position-live/results.md`; expected results (written first): `evidence/fine-position-live/expected-phone.md`.

The criterion: show the provisional offense position and calculated fine after the person and ordinance are identified; show unavailable calculations instead of zero. The roadmap line also asked to keep the paper-recorded penalty separately and flag discrepancies. **That part was dropped by user decision:** the system is the source for what the enforcer writes on the paper ticket, so there is no separate paper amount to compare. It was not built and is not counted as passed.

## Finding

The offense position, the fine, the total and the "unavailable" messages already existed (ordinance revision slices, person slice, faster entry). One gap was real: the fine on the entry page is provisional. The enforcer copies it onto the paper, then Verify works it out again on the server. If the server's answer differed, the Review page showed the new amount with "Verified." and never said it differed from what had been copied.

## What was added

- **Phone, one new notice** (`fine_changed_notice.dart`, shown by `new_inspection_preview_page.dart` after Verify): "The fine changed after you copied it. Write ₱X on the paper ticket instead of ₱Y." For a cash fine only. If an offense position changed: "Section 12 is now the 2nd offense, not the 1st." It only warns; Submit stays available. No fine line for blood donation or community service.
- **Tests** (phone, 11 cases): `fine_summary_display_test.dart` (4): the entry and Review pages show a 2nd and 3rd offense with the right fines and total (2nd ₱500.00 + 3rd ₱1,500.00 = ₱2,000.00, worked out by hand); a line with no confirmed penalty makes the total unavailable, not a partial total or zero; a missing scoring severity is not shown as a fine or as Minor. `fine_changed_notice_test.dart` (7): the notice says nothing when nothing changed, names the amount to write when the fine changed, names a changed position when the fine is the same, says nothing about a fine for blood donation, says nothing before Verify has answered; on the Review page it appears after Verify when the server fine differs (Submit still on) and does not appear when the server agrees.
- **Server, website, database schema, API contract:** no change.
